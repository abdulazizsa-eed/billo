Undo and redo with two stacks

Type in the editor, then undo and redo. Each edit is pushed onto the undo stack; undoing pops it and pushes it onto the redo stack. The top of each stack is outlined.

    Redo stack

      1. A new edit is applied, pushed onto the undo stack, and clears the redo stack.
      2. Undo pops the undo stack, reverses the edit, and pushes it onto the redo stack.
      3. Redo pops the redo stack, re-applies the edit, and pushes it onto the undo stack.

      Shortcuts: Ctrl/Cmd+Z to undo, Ctrl+Y or Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z to redo.
